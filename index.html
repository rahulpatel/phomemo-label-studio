    Design in Figma, print from here

    1. In Figma, make a frame the shape of your label (for example 400 × 240 for 50 × 30 mm).
    2. For text that changes, type a placeholder like {{name}} or {{date}}.
    3. Select the frame, then right-click → Copy/Paste as → Copy as SVG, and paste here with ⌘V.
      Or export it as SVG (keep "Outline text" off) and drop the file here.
    4. Fill in the fields on the right and press Print.

    Paper & alignment

    Prints a dashed box 3 mm in from each edge. If it's off-center, shift by half the difference. Edges the print head can't reach are hatched red in the preview.

    Label sizes

    Width is across the printer (max 50 mm, 48 mm printable). Height is the length of each label.
